# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Screenwriters blocking out scenes and hobbyists/beginners who want structure without jargon. They're creative people working on a laptop or desktop, in coffee shops, home offices, or writing retreats. They want speed — the app should never get between them and the words. They may know Fountain syntax or be discovering it for the first time.

## Product Purpose

A story-building app that makes the three-act structure feel natural, not academic. Writers create acts, populate them with scenes, and write in Markdown or Fountain — with a live rendered preview always visible. The app is local-first, loads instantly, and exports to Markdown, PDF, and Fountain. No accounts, no servers, no friction.

## Brand Personality

playful, modern, fast

The tone is warm and encouraging but never precious. The interface responds instantly — every keystroke, every drag, every toggle. The visual personality is unique but restrained: creative tools should feel like a workshop, not a toy store.

## Anti-references

- No clutter. Every element earns its place.
- No corporate sterility (Salesforce, SAP, boring B2B dashboards).
- No clone energy — the point is to be distinct, not "Notion for screenwriters" or "another writing tool."
- No skeuomorphic "leather-bound notebook" or "typewriter" tropes. This is a modern tool for modern writers.

## Design Principles

1. **Words come first.** The editor is king. Navigation, chrome, and settings recede behind the act of writing.
2. **Instant response.** Every action completes in the same frame. Autosave is silent. Drag-and-drop is fluid. The app never waits.
3. **Structure as guide, not cage.** The three-act scaffold is a starting point — users can add, rename, reorder freely. The framework serves the story, not the other way around.
4. **Same content, multiple faces.** Write in Markdown/Fountain once; see it rendered, preview it as a screenplay, export to any format. The source is always the source.
5. **Delight without decoration.** Personality lives in motion, typography, and thoughtful micro-interactions — not in ornament, gradients, or illustrations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA as the baseline.
- Keyboard-navigable: every action reachable without a mouse.
- Dark and light modes, persisted.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout.
- Font sizes and contrast tuned for extended reading sessions.
